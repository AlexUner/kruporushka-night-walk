# Expanded route map concepts

The generated board shows three alternatives. The implementation takes the wide cartography and photo stops from A because both remain visible on desktop and stack without overlap on a phone. B supplies the circular entry radar, where a square panel would take more room from the photographs. C inspired the opening transition, while the final map keeps its stop links visible rather than placing them behind a drawer.

The design uses the existing photo assets, exact scene names and user-provided coordinates. The wider map is drawn from OpenStreetMap geometry with a custom palette. No generated building, street or route lines are used. The document structure includes a clear title and native links in both the large map and the photo list. The scan and shared transition respect reduced motion.
